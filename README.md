AI Career & Skills Alignment Assistant (CareerFit AI)An AI-powered, document-grounded assistant that matches fresh graduates and job seekers with relevant career opportunities and identifies skill gaps using official occupational frameworks.Problem StatementFresh graduates and job seekers frequently face skill-related underemployment due to a lack of visibility into real-world job requirements and skill expectations. Conversely, employers receive vast volumes of misaligned applications. CareerFit AI provides a data-grounded, 24/7 intelligent assistant that evaluates user qualifications against national occupational standards and identifies exact upskilling needs.Evidence of the ProblemUnderemployment: Official statistics indicate persistent skill-related underemployment among tertiary graduates due to structural mismatches between education outputs and industry needs (DOSM Graduates Statistics Report).Rapidly Evolving Skill Demands: Core job skills are changing rapidly across industries, creating a widening gap between academic preparation and market expectations (World Economic Forum Future of Jobs Report).Initial Related Work / Existing ApproachesJob Portals (LinkedIn / JobStreet): Dependent on keyword searches and lack structured skills gap analysis grounded in national occupational standards.General LLMs (ChatGPT): Flexible for drafting resumes, but prone to hallucinations and ungrounded career advice.University Advisory Centers: High quality guidance, but constrained by human bandwidth and operational hours.Engineering Gap / LimitationCurrent solutions lack a transparent, document-grounded mechanism that maps individual academic profiles directly to standardized national occupational frameworks (e.g., MASCO / COL) to provide hallucination-free career alignment and upskilling guidance.Project ObjectivesO1: Design a document-grounded AI alignment assistant based on official workforce and occupational frameworks.O2: Develop the retrieval pipeline with domain-specific guardrails to ensure grounded recommendations.O3: Evaluate the system on retrieval precision, groundedness, latency, and fairness.Target UsersFresh university graduates seeking entry-level positions matched to their skills.Early-career professionals and career switchers looking for targeted upskilling pathways.Data SourcesTalentCorp Critical Occupations List (COL): https://www.talentcorp.com.myMalaysia Standard Classification of Occupations (MASCO):
